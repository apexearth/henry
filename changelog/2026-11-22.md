# 2026-11-22

- Whisper's prompt now carries the names from the conversation in view (backticked identifiers, camelCase, capitalised names, up to 24) and from Henry's spoken replies, so a word already on screen is heard and spelled as written; a question to Henry is primed by the session in view too
- Background noise no longer comes out as "Thanks for watching": those video sign-offs and captions like "(dramatic music)" are dropped from a transcript, and `voice.vadModel` (a Silero model) has whisper hear only the parts of a clip with speech in them
- A message from another session or a coordinator no longer lands in the middle of what you are typing: it waits while a key was pressed in the last few seconds or Claude Code's input box (read off the screen) holds a draft, and goes in once you send or clear it
- On a phone, talk mode keeps the screen from locking, and a lock no longer leaves voice dead until the app is killed: a mic that stops sending audio resets itself and says to tap again, and a transcription left hanging across the lock is retried
