# 2026-11-20

- A topic's members answer their coordinator: when a turn the coordinator's prompt started ends, the member's last reply goes into the coordinator's input, submitted between its turns, up to twelve an hour per topic and as drafts after that; turns you start in a member stay yours
- A topic can hold sessions on several machines: a session on any machine paired both ways with the topic's can join it from the rail, gets the brief on its prompts, shows in the coordinator's view with what it was asked and wrote, takes the coordinator's prompts and sends its replies back over the link, and leaves when the topic closes
- Right-click menus stay on screen: opened near the bottom or right edge they flip up or left, one taller than the window scrolls, and Esc closes them
- Under "by topic", a session in a topic on another machine is listed in that topic, under the topic's machine and tagged with its own, instead of under its own machine
- A chime lights the rail row of the session it is about, amber when it needs you and green when it finished, fading over a few seconds, so with several sessions finishing you can see which one it was
- Sessions on a machine paired with others get henry_fetch_file, which copies a file from a paired machine (by machine, or by a session there for a relative path) into the session's folder; the other machine is only read, and an existing file is kept unless overwrite is asked for
