# 2026-10-15

- The files tree follows the disk: the daemon watches the working trees a files pane shows, so a file Claude or anything else makes, moves or deletes there appears or leaves within a second, and the tree reads everything again on window focus
- The files tree shows what .gitignore hides, dimmed (`.claude`, `node_modules` and the like open on demand), and empty folders; ⊘ hides ignored files, ⊟ closes every folder
- The files tree's right-click menu makes folders, renames (F2) and moves within a root, deletes to the Recycle Bin or Trash after asking, copies a path or relative path, and shows a file in Explorer or Finder
- A repo a session has not worked in for 8 hours, or that you hid from it, folds under "earlier" at the bottom of the files tree, and a removed worktree leaves the tree and the repo cards for good
