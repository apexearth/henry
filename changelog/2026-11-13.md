# 2026-11-13

- Continuous dictation types sentences instead of "make it... Better": each clip is transcribed as the rest of what you just said, a pause's "..." is dropped, and the full stop whisper puts at every cut waits for the next clip to show whether the sentence really ended
- Dictation no longer fails when whisper takes more than 10 s on a busy machine: voice requests are exempt from Bun's idle cutoff, whisper gets two minutes, the window retries a failed clip, and one that still fails is kept behind a "retry" button rather than lost
- Voice no longer gets stuck until the app is restarted: every microphone and audio-device wait has a timeout, a context that will not start is rebuilt, and a strip stuck in one state resets itself and says where it was stuck
- On a phone, tapping or holding the talk slab dictates continuously: the words land in the box at each pause while you keep talking, instead of all at once when you stop
- Hiding git-ignored files (`⊘`) also hides folders that hold only ignored files; they were listed as empty folders and stayed in the tree
