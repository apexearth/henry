# 2026-11-09

- The window chimes when a session needs you (rising) or finished (falling), from the same notices as the OS notification, with its own tick in Settings, so a global `afplay` hook is no longer needed
- Henry can say what finished: with "say what it did or wants" ticked, a few seconds after the chime Haiku reads the end of that session's conversation and Henry speaks one line, the result or what it needs from you, which also becomes the notification's text
- A session whose turn ended while its background agent keeps running no longer pulses as working: the agent's tool calls after the turn are heartbeats, and ending a turn of real work into background work is now a notice, since that turn often ended by asking you something
- Stopping dictation with the button or the key hands the keyboard back to the session in view, so Enter sends what you said without clicking into the terminal first
